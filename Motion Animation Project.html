Garfield covers a 50 m route in four 8-second sections. Each section has its own velocity and acceleration, and the graphs below show how they add up to one trip.

The route runs from 0 m to 50 m. Numbered flags mark where each section ends, and the dashed line follows Garfield.

Within a section, v = v₀ + aτ and x = x₀ + v₀τ + ½aτ², where τ is the time since the section began.

At 12 seconds, halfway through section 2, the story changes when Garfield spots Nermal. The motion does not change at that moment. Acceleration stays at −0.3 m/s², and the dashed line on each graph marks the time.

Velocity jumps at 8, 16 and 24 seconds because each section sets its own starting velocity. The graphs leave a gap at each jump.
